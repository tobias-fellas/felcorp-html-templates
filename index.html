1. Key Overview

1 per article Position: top Embed block Wraps in data-rt-embed-type with its own <style> block.
Key Points

Business process outsourcing (BPO) enables organisations to offload functions to specialist providers, improving efficiency, scalability and focus on strategic priorities.

  • BPO covers both back-office and front-office functions and can be delivered onshore, nearshore or offshore depending on business and regulatory needs
  • Modern BPO is a strategic capability, providing access to specialised talent and flexible operating capacity
  • The strongest BPO outcomes come from clear governance, defined SLAs and providers with proven industry expertise
  • Risks such as data security and quality control can be effectively mitigated with the right structure and oversight

2. Tip / Message Overview

Up to 2 per article No embed wrapper Standalone RTE Direct in the Rich Text Element. No data-rt-embed-type wrapper needed.

Variation A: Tip lead-in

Tip: We always recommend you trial a BPO service before a formal commitment. There is simply no way you can be sure a provider will work for you without trialing their service first.

Variation B: Important lead-in

Important: Not all KPIs should be SLAs. Turning every KPI into a contractual obligation makes the model brittle and increases escalation friction.

Variation C: Plain text (no lead-in)

At Felcorp Support, we do not have exit fees in our contract. However, we do have a minimum 3 month initial engagement period that adequately covers our up-front set up costs.

3. Table (bpo-table)

1-2 per article Embed block Column widths adjustable Wraps in data-rt-embed-type with its own <style> block.

2-column layout (32% / 68%)

SLA ElementWhat it means in practice
Service scopeDefines exactly what the provider is responsible for delivering.
Performance metricsSets measurable standards such as accuracy or turnaround time.
Measurement methodExplains how data will be tracked and validated.
ConsequencesDescribes what happens when SLAs are not met including credits or actions.

3-column layout (25% / 35% / 40%)

DimensionWhat to MeasureExample Metric
QualityAccuracy, compliance adherence, rework rateError rate below 2% after calibration
SpeedTurnaround time, SLA adherence95% of tasks within agreed SLA window
CostCost per unit vs. in-house baselineWithin 15% of in-house equivalent
SatisfactionStakeholder confidence, communication qualityRating of 4/5 or above
